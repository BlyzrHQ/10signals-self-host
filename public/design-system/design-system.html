Principles

01

Say what was checked and what was not. "Not observed", never 0. "Needs evidence", never a guess.

02

Provenance is shape plus fill, never color alone: ● observed, ◐ inferred, ◔ limited, ○ unavailable.

03

One blue. No red/green judgement. Favourable vs unfavourable is words and position.

04

Every recommendation carries its origin: AI-drafted or Rule-based. Every claim ends in a source and a date.

05

Not-live functionality wears a dashed "Planned" pill. Nothing is presented as shipped that isn't.

06

Bilingual by default: logical properties, dir="ltr" on numbers, prices, domains and URLs.
